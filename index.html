Afterink in about two minutes

A contract repository for the stage after signature, built for an invented company, Kestrel Mobility. Everything you click works, and nothing is sent to anyone.

  1. FileLegal Ops files a signed document. AI reads it and code checks it.
  2. CheckAccounts Payable checks invoices against the agreed rates.
  3. DecideProcurement decides each renewal before its notice date.
  4. MeasureSuccess is dates decided in time, not alerts sent.

You can restart the tour any time from the top bar.
